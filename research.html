Research & Projects

REYES program — Lattice QCD (Jun 2024 – Sep 2024)

Analyzed two-point correlation functions to extract pion energies across momentum channels and compared dispersion with theoretical predictions. Implemented fitting pipelines and uncertainty estimates.

Structure functions — IISER Pune (Jan 2023 – Jul 2023)

Worked with PDFs and DGLAP evolution in FORTRAN to model nucleon structure functions and perform scale evolution tests.

ML for neutrino detection — IceCube (Jan 2023 – Mar 2023)

Built detection models and evaluated performance; focused on feature engineering and class imbalance strategies.
