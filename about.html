Background

I am a Master's graduate in Physics from Pune University (2023), with a specialization in Experimental High Energy Physics. My passion lies in understanding the fundamental nature of the universe, particularly through the search for new physics beyond the Standard Model. I have a strong foundation in physics, coupled with hands-on experience in scientific computing, data analysis, and programming.

My journey into physics research began with an undergraduate project at JNCASR, Bangalore, where I synthesized the exotic YBCO superconductor. This experience sparked my curiosity about the fundamental principles governing matter and forces. I then honed my research skills during my Master's thesis at IISER-P, focusing on the statistical modeling of nucleon structure functions. This work involved using the QCDNUM library in Fortran to analyze proton-proton collision data and plot Parton Distribution Functions (PDFs), demonstrating my proficiency in high-energy physics, quantum chromodynamics (QCD), and scientific computing.

Beyond my academic work, I'm a strong advocate for applying my knowledge to real-world challenges. In 2023, I participated in a Kaggle competition to enhance neutrino detection efficiency at the IceCube Neutrino Observatory. Collaborating with a diverse team, I contributed to the development of deep learning models using frameworks like PyTorch. This experience not only strengthened my technical skills in machine learning and data analysis but also underscored the value of effective teamwork in scientific research. I am driven by a desire to contribute to cutting-edge research and am eager to apply my skills to new challenges.
